Thirteen days, two stories

October 1962, as told by Washington and by Moscow, and what both of their records confirm.

5 scenes. Scroll to play, or press Watch.

Two governments told two different stories about why the world came close to nuclear war. Each line below cites its source, and each interpretation says whose it is.

16 Oct 1962

October 1962. Photographs from a U-2 flight over Cuba show missile sites under construction, about 90 miles from Florida.

Oct 1962

Two sets of missiles sit on two doorsteps: Soviet missiles in Cuba, and American Jupiter missiles near İzmir in Turkey.

22 Oct 1962

In Washington's telling, the crisis was caused by the Soviet missiles in Cuba. President Kennedy says so on television.

27 Oct 1962

In Moscow's telling, the cause was the American missiles in Turkey. Khrushchev puts it plainly in a letter.

Apr 1963

What both sides confirm: the crisis ended with the Soviet missiles leaving Cuba, and the Jupiters left Turkey by April 1963.
