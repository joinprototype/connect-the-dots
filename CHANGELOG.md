# Changelog

What changed in each version of the site. [docs/deploy.md](docs/deploy.md#versions) says when the number goes up.

## 0.1.2 (2026-10-30)

- Ask: suggestions now open right under the field. The browser's own list could open far away, even over another window.

## 0.1.1 (2026-10-30)

- Search engines may now list the site. Preview links stay out of them.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-30)

The first version online, at [dots.joinprototype.com](https://dots.joinprototype.com). Search engines are asked not to list it yet.

- 378 entries, 742 sourced links and 540 sources, each link showing its proof
- Ask: routes between two things, and answers to "who is behind", "who benefited" and "why did it happen"
- Four illustrated stories that play by scrolling or by Listen, with maps that show the borders of their date
- The explorer and the Map, from one dot to the whole Earth
- Made for phones as well as laptops
- Open: the code and data are public, and [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) says on what terms
