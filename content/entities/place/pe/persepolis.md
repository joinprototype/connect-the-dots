Xerxes’s Gate of All Nations inscription describes building at Persepolis by the favor of Ahuramazda. Darius I’s Behistun title is about two centuries older than Alexander’s invasion and describes royal authority in its own terms; it does not report the campaign. See [XPa](https://www.livius.org/sources/content/achaemenid-royal-inscriptions/xpa/) and [Behistun I.1–3](https://www.livius.org/sources/content/behistun-persian-text/behistun-t-01/).
