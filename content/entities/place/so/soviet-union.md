The Union of Soviet Socialist Republics, ruled from [[moscow]] by the [[soviet-government]] from 1922 to 1991.

It was founded on 30 December 1922 ([[founding-of-the-ussr]]) and dissolved in December 1991 ([[dissolution-of-the-soviet-union]]).
