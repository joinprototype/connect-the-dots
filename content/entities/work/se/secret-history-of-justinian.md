Paul Halsall's introduction describes [[procopius]]'s Secret History as an attack on Justinian and Theodora, written around 550 and published after its author's death. Procopius says fear of spies and death had forced him to conceal causes in his earlier history; here he calls [[justinian-i]] a second plague.
