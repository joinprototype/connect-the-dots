Plutarch wrote in the late first or early second century CE, roughly 400–450 years after Alexander. He explicitly uses Aristobulus, Chares and Onesicritus, whose complete accounts are lost, and letters he attributes to Alexander. He often preserves alternatives rather than reconciling them. See [chapters 15–16](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Plutarch/Lives/Alexander*/3.html), [chapter 24](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Plutarch/Lives/Alexander*/4.html) and [Heckel, pp. 9–10](https://web.archive.org/web/2024/https://assets.cambridge.org/97805218/42471/excerpt/9780521842471_excerpt.pdf).
