name: "Adamson Act"
summary: "1916 U.S. law that made eight hours the pay basis for a day’s work for covered interstate train crews."
when: "1916-09-03"
---

The act set January 1, 1917, as the start of the eight-hour standard. During the period set aside to study the change and 30 days after it, employers had to preserve the existing day’s wage and pay at least the proportional rate for necessary work beyond eight hours.
