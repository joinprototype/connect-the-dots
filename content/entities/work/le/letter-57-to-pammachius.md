[[jerome]] asks Pammachius to judge his Latin rendering of Epiphanius’s letter against the Greek. He defends translating meaning through examples from scripture, including a comparison of [[abraham]]’s purchase of a burial place in Genesis and the account in Acts. Section 11 compares the [[septuagint]] with Hebrew and Jerome’s own version.
