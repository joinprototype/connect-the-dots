The [[second-all-russia-congress-of-soviets|Second All-Russia Congress of Soviets]] adopted the decree in Petrograd. It took its land terms from a mandate the national peasant soviet’s newspaper compiled from 242 local mandates and placed landed estates at the disposal of local land committees and peasant soviets.
