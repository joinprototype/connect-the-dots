name: "Fair Labor Standards Act"
summary: "U.S. law that established federal minimum wages and phased in a 40-hour overtime threshold for covered workers."
when: "1938-06-25"
---

The original act set the weekly threshold at 44 hours, then 42, then 40 starting October 24, 1940. It required at least time and a half for additional hours. Its original coverage left out household service in private homes, and it explicitly exempted agricultural workers from its wage and hours rules.
