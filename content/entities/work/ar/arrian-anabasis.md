Arrian wrote roughly 450 years after Alexander. In his preface he names Ptolemy and Aristobulus as his preferred authorities because both accompanied the expedition; he says both wrote after Alexander’s death. Their complete histories are lost. Waldemar Heckel treats this as a tradition defending the king and omitting damaging episodes. See [Arrian’s preface](https://archive.org/download/L236ArrianIAnabasisOfAlexander14/L236-Arrian%20I%20Anabasis%20of%20Alexander%201-4_djvu.txt) and [Heckel, pp. 8–10](https://web.archive.org/web/2024/https://assets.cambridge.org/97805218/42471/excerpt/9780521842471_excerpt.pdf).
