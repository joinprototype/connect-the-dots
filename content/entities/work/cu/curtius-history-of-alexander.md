On the usual first-century CE dating, Curtius wrote roughly 350–400 years after Alexander; Waldemar Heckel discusses the uncertain identification of the author. Heckel identifies Cleitarchus as his main lost source, with additional material from Ptolemy. The quotation about the Persepolis fire is from surviving Book V, chapter VII, not the supplements by Freinshemius printed in the same volume. See [the English edition, pp. 289–291](https://archive.org/details/quintuscurtiushi00curt) and [Heckel, p. 9](https://web.archive.org/web/2024/https://assets.cambridge.org/97805218/42471/excerpt/9780521842471_excerpt.pdf).
