In his 1909 memoir, [[john-d-rockefeller]] acknowledged railroad rebates before 1880 and argued that Standard paid for its advantages with services that lowered hauling costs. He described regular traffic, loading and terminal facilities, and assuming the risk of fire; he also recalled pipeline operators remitting part of a through charge to railroads. These are his retrospective explanations in [“The Question of Rebates” and “Pipelines vs. Railroads”](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/17090).
