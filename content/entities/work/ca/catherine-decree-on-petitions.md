name: "Catherine II’s decree on serfs’ petitions"
summary: "A 1767 decree prescribing punishment for unlawful petitions against landowners."
when: "1767-08"
---

The decree singled out unlawful petitions addressed to the empress personally and ordered petitioners and those who wrote for them whipped and sent to penal servitude for life. People sent away under the decree counted toward their landlords’ army recruit quotas.
