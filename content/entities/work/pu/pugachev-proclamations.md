The first manifesto invoked service to former tsars. His proclamation to former landlord peasants offered freedom from landlords and ordered the execution of estate nobles he described as enemies of his power. The University of North Carolina Greensboro edition dates the proclamation to June 1774 and the Free Economic Society dates it to late July.
