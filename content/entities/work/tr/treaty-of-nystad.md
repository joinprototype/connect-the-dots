name: "Treaty of Nystad"
summary: "The peace treaty that ended the Great Northern War in 1721."
when: "1721-09-10"
---

The Swedish crown gave up Livonia, Estonia, Ingria and part of Karelia, among other territories. Peter agreed to return most of Finland. Russia celebrated the treaty at Holy Trinity Cathedral in Saint Petersburg, where Peter received the title of emperor.
