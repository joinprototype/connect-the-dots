The Jupiter was an intermediate-range ballistic missile developed by the Army Ballistic Missile Agency under [[wernher-von-braun]]. The [[us-government]] stationed Jupiters in Italy and [[turkey]] ([[jupiter-deployment-turkey]]) and removed them in 1963 ([[jupiter-withdrawal-1963]]).
