Under the U.S. tax code a taxpayer who itemizes may deduct gifts to charities, within limits set by income. [[us-congress]] created it in 1917, as Rob Reich tells it ([[rob-reich]]). The Treasury counts the revenue it costs as a tax expenditure, in three lines for education, health and everything else.
