name: "Donor-advised fund"
summary: "An account at a charity that a donor funds and advises but no longer owns."
---

The IRS describes it as an account kept by a sponsoring charity, which holds legal control, while the donor keeps the right to advise on grants and investments. [[donorstrust]] provides them.
