"In God we trust" became the national motto of the [[united-states]] by a joint resolution that [[dwight-d-eisenhower]] signed on 30 July 1956.

Why it was adopted then is a matter of interpretation. [[politifact]], summarizing historians, links the 1950s religious phrases to Cold War contrast with the officially atheist Soviet Union ([[soviet-state-atheism]]).
