The 1922 Union Treaty stated: "Each of the united Republics retains its full liberty to leave the Union if it so desires." In December 1991 the republics used that right to dissolve the [[soviet-union]].

According to the [[russian-government]], this right was "the mine" laid under the Soviet state and the cause of its [[dissolution-of-the-soviet-union|disintegration]].
