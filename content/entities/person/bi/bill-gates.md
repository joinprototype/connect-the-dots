Bill Gates co-founded [[microsoft]] with [[paul-allen]] in 1975, in [[albuquerque]]. He later turned to philanthropy.

In 2010 he, Melinda French Gates and [[warren-buffett]] started the [[giving-pledge]], which [[elon-musk]] joined in 2012.

He is the son of the Seattle lawyer [[william-h-gates-sr]] and of [[mary-maxwell-gates]], a United Way national board member. He learned to program at [[lakeside-school]], where the Mothers Club paid for a computer terminal, and left [[harvard-university]] in 1975. In 1980 Microsoft won the contract for the [[ibm-pc]]'s operating system, the [[ibm-pc-dos-deal]].
