Jackie Bezos, born Jacklyn Gise, was the daughter of [[lawrence-preston-gise]] and became a mother at 17. She married [[mike-bezos]] when her son [[jeff-bezos]] was four.

In 1995 she and Mike Bezos were among the first outside investors in [[amazon]]: Amazon's 1997 prospectus records share purchases worth about $245,573. With her husband she founded the Bezos Family Foundation, which works on early learning. She died in August 2025.
