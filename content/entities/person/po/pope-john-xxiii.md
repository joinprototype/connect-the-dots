Angelo Giuseppe Roncalli was elected pope in October 1958 and took the name John XXIII. He headed the [[catholic-church]] until his death in June 1963.

On 25 October 1962, during the [[cuban-missile-crisis]], he made a radio appeal for peace to world leaders. In April 1963 he issued the encyclical [[pacem-in-terris]].
