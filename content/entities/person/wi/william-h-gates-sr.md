William H. Gates Sr. was a Seattle lawyer. In 1964 he co-founded the partnership that became Preston Gates & Ellis (today K&L Gates), and he served as president of the Washington State Bar Association. He married [[mary-maxwell-gates]] in 1951; their son is [[bill-gates]].

From 2000 to 2020 he was a co-chair of his son's foundation.
