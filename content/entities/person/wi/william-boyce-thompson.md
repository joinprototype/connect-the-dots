William Boyce Thompson led the American Red Cross mission in Petrograd in 1917. According to [[antony-sutton]], he gave $1 million to the [[bolsheviks]]. US embassy records from December 1917 instead connect American Red Cross money with propaganda for the [[russian-provisional-government]]'s side. Both claims are shown with their sources.
