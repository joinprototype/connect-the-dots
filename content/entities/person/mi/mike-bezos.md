Mike Bezos came to the United States from Cuba as a teenager, studied at the University of Albuquerque and became an engineer for Exxon. He married [[jackie-bezos]] when [[jeff-bezos]] was four.

In February 1995 he bought Amazon shares for about $100,020, part of the roughly $245,573 that Jeff Bezos's parents put into [[amazon]] that year.
