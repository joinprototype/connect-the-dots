Sindona was a financier who advised the Vatican in the 1960s. TIME reported in 1982 that he introduced [[roberto-calvi]] to [[paul-marcinkus]] in 1971. He was convicted in New York in 1980 and in Milan in 1986, and died in an Italian prison on 22 March 1986.
