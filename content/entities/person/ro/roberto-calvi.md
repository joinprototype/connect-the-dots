Calvi was president of [[banco-ambrosiano]] when it failed in 1982. He was found hanging beneath [[blackfriars-bridge]] on 18 June 1982. Whether he was murdered has been before British and Italian courts; see [[calvi-inquests]] and [[calvi-murder-trial]].
