Chairman of Standard Oil of Indiana from 1918 until the vote of 7 March 1929, by the Whiting-Robertsdale Historical Society’s account. It says Liberty bonds from the Continental Trading Company were traced to him, that he acknowledged holding them, and that he was never charged or prosecuted in connection with that scandal or with Teapot Dome.
