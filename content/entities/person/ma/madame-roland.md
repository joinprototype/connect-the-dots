Ida M. Tarbell recounts that she asked her lawyer Chauveau-Lagarde to stay away rather than risk his life without saving hers. Tarbell describes her written defence denying the accusations against her friends, the Tribunal’s refusal to let her read it, and her conviction and execution on 8 November 1793. [Tarbell, chapter XIII](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/63699).
