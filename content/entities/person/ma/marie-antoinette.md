Ferrières records her agreement to face the Versailles crowd despite the danger on 6 October 1789. Her farewell to Élisabeth maintains her innocence, pardons her enemies and asks her son never to avenge his parents. She was executed on 16 October 1793. [Ferrières](https://revolution.chnm.org/d/309), [last letter in Yonge, chapter LVII](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/10555), [execution date](https://revolution.chnm.org/d/1051).

The cake remark is a popular legend about her. John M. Cunningham’s Britannica account finds no historical evidence for attributing it to her and traces an earlier version to Rousseau’s unnamed princess. [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/story/did-marie-antoinette-really-say-let-them-eat-cake).
