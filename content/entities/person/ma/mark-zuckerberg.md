Mark Zuckerberg went from Phillips Exeter Academy to [[harvard-university]], where in February 2004 he launched Thefacebook from his dormitory. He left Harvard and has led [[meta-platforms|Facebook]] since its incorporation in July 2004.

His first outside investor was [[peter-thiel]]; in 2005 [[accel]] and [[jim-breyer]] followed, and in 2007 [[microsoft]] bought a stake. In his 2017 Harvard commencement speech he said: "If I had to support my family growing up instead of having time to code, if I didn't know I'd be fine if Facebook didn't work out, I wouldn't be standing here today."
