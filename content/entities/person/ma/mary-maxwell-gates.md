Mary Maxwell Gates came from a Seattle banking family; her father and grandfather were prominent bankers. She was a University of Washington regent for 18 years, the first woman director of First Interstate Bank of Washington, and in 1983 the first woman to chair the national [[united-way-of-america|United Way]]'s executive committee.

On that national board she served with IBM's president, [[john-opel]]. According to [[the-seattle-times]], she "helped cement Microsoft's early connection with IBM", the [[ibm-pc-dos-deal|1980 deal]] that put her son's company in the IBM PC.
