Serhii Plokhy teaches at [[harvard-university]]. His book *The Last Empire* (2014) argues that the Soviet Union ended because Russia and Ukraine could not agree to keep a common state, and that the decisive moment was the [[ukrainian-independence-referendum-1991|Ukrainian referendum]] of 1 December 1991, not American policy.
