Lawrence Preston Gise was a career US government employee. He headed the [[atomic-energy-commission]]'s operations for a seven-state region from [[albuquerque]], then retired early to his family's ranch in Cotulla, Texas.

He was the father of [[jackie-bezos]] and the maternal grandfather of [[jeff-bezos]]. Accounts of the Bezos family also place him at the newly created [[darpa|ARPA]] in 1958; that link is still a draft until it is checked against a primary record.
