Jeff Bezos was born in [[albuquerque]]. He founded [[amazon]] in 1994 and was its chief executive from 1996 to 2021, and he founded the spaceflight company [[blue-origin]] in 2000.

His maternal grandfather, [[lawrence-preston-gise]], headed [[atomic-energy-commission]] operations in Albuquerque. Blue Origin builds a lunar lander for [[nasa]].

His mother [[jackie-bezos]] is Gise's daughter; his father [[mike-bezos]] married her when Jeff was four. After [[princeton-university]] he worked on Wall Street, from 1990 to 1994 at [[d-e-shaw]]. Amazon's first outside money came from his parents, about $245,573 in 1995, and its first venture capital from [[kleiner-perkins]] in 1996.
