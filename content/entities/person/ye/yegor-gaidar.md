Yegor Gaidar led Russia's first market reforms in the [[russian-government]]. In his book *The Collapse of an Empire* (2006) and a 2006 lecture he traced the Soviet collapse to grain imports and the [[oil-price-collapse-1986|1985–86 oil price collapse]].
