Cuitláhuac succeeded [[moctezuma-ii|Moctezuma]] as ruler of [[tenochtitlan|Tenochtitlan]] in 1520. [[bernal-diaz-del-castillo|Bernal Díaz]] says he died of smallpox and [[cuauhtemoc|Cuauhtémoc]] took his place.
