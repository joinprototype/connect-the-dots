Stanislav Shushkevich chaired the parliament of [[belarus]] and hosted the [[belovezha-accords]]. In 2016 he told RFE/RL that the Soviet Union had essentially collapsed after the [[august-coup-1991]], and that the accords avoided a "Yugoslav scenario".
