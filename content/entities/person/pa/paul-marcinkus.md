Marcinkus was president of the [[institute-for-the-works-of-religion]] during the years it dealt with [[roberto-calvi]], and also ran the city-state's administration. Italian magistrates issued warrants for him in 1987; the Court of Cassation invalidated them for lack of Italian jurisdiction. He was never tried. A press report on the Vatican's papers says he resigned two years after 1987; the Los Angeles Times says he resigned in 1990.
