Peter Thiel co-founded [[paypal]] in 1998, where he crossed paths with [[elon-musk]], and became [[meta-platforms|Facebook]]'s first outside investor in 2004: $500,000 for 10.2%. He sat on Facebook's board from April 2005 to May 2022.

In 2003 he co-founded [[palantir-technologies]], which built software for US intelligence and counted [[in-q-tel]], the venture firm set up by the [[cia]], among its early backers. These are documented business links; no source cited here says the CIA had any role in Facebook.
