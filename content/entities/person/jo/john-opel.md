John Opel spent four decades at [[ibm]]: president from 1974, chief executive from 1981 to 1985. The IBM PC was launched as he became CEO.

He also sat on the national board of the [[united-way-of-america|United Way]] with [[mary-maxwell-gates]]. When Microsoft was mentioned to him in 1980, the Seattle Times reported, he answered: "Oh, that's run by Bill Gates, Mary Gates' son."
