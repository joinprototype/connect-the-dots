The seven were Standard Oil (New Jersey), Standard Oil of California, Socony-Vacuum, Gulf and Texaco, with the British-Dutch Anglo-Iranian and Royal Dutch-Shell. The report concerns the world market, not the United States alone, and says it contains no recommendations.
