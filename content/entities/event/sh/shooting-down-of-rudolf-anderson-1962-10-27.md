On Saturday 27 October 1962 a surface-to-air missile fired from [[cuba]] downed a U-2 flown by [[rudolf-anderson]]. The National Security Archive says Soviet generals in Cuba gave the order to the missile site at Banes. See [[cuban-missile-crisis]].
