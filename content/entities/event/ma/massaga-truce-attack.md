Diodorus and Plutarch describe an attack after a truce; Arrian explains it as a response to planned desertion. Welles’s notes to Diodorus 17.84 identify the city as Massaga and set out the disagreement. See [[alexanders-asian-campaigns]], [[alexander-the-great]], [[diodorus-library-of-history]], [[plutarch-life-of-alexander]] and [[arrian-anabasis]].
