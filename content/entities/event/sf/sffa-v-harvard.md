The district court ruled for Harvard on the race claim; the Supreme Court reversed in 2023. The opinion describes how recruited athletes, legacies, applicants on the dean’s or director’s interest lists, and children of faculty and staff are treated in Harvard’s admissions, and why Harvard says it does so.
