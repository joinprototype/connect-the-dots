Operation Anadyr was the Soviet General Staff's code name for the secret 1962 deployment of nuclear-capable missiles, aircraft and troops to [[cuba]], agreed by [[nikita-khrushchev]] and [[fidel-castro]] in July 1962. Its discovery by the United States began the [[cuban-missile-crisis]].

The name, after a river and town in the Soviet Far North, was chosen to hide the destination.
