On the night of 27 October 1962 the Soviet submarine [[submarine-b-59]] had to surface near U.S. ships hunting it. Its captain, Valentin Savitsky, according to crew members, ordered a dive and called for the nuclear torpedo; [[vasily-arkhipov]], standing on the conning tower, helped him stand down. The Americans did not know of the torpedo.
