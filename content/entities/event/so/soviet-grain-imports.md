From 1963 the [[soviet-union]] bought grain abroad. In his 2006 lecture at the American Enterprise Institute, [[yegor-gaidar]] counted these imports, paid for in hard currency, among the causes of the collapse. See [[dissolution-of-the-soviet-union]].
