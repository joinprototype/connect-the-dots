[[alaric]]'s army took the city. [[marcella]]'s story is in [[letter-127-to-principia]]. [[augustine-of-hippo]] answered with [[city-of-god]].

J. B. Bury dates entry to 24 August 410 and gives the sack two or three days; Orosius says the army left on the third day, while Gibbon gives six days. Jerome describes residents killed and made captive. Augustine writes of killing, plunder, burning, captivity and rape, and tells survivors that violence against their bodies was no fault of theirs.
