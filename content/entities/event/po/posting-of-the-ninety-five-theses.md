Philip Melanchthon wrote in 1546 that [[martin-luther]] posted the [[ninety-five-theses]] on a church door at [[wittenberg]]. Luther's own accounts speak of letters to bishops, among them [[albert-of-brandenburg]]. Scholars give both sides.
