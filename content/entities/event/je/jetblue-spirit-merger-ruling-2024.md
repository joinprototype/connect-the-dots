On 16 January 2024 a federal judge ruled that JetBlue’s agreed purchase of Spirit would violate Section 7 of the Clayton Act and enjoined it. The airlines had argued Spirit was struggling financially and the merged firm would be a stronger rival to the big four; the court said JetBlue was far from the only available purchaser should Spirit fall into dire need. Spirit stopped flying in May 2026.
