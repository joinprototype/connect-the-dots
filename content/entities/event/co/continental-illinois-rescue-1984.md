In May 1984 a run on Continental Illinois led the FDIC, the Fed and the Comptroller to a rescue that covered all depositors. At House hearings in September and October a member said the rule had created “a TBTF—too big to fail”; the Comptroller conceded regulators had no way yet to let a large bank fail.
