The Washington Post reported in 2014 that the Gates Foundation spent more than $200 million, as Education Week quotes it, building political support for the standards. Bill Gates said the foundation funds people to look into things, not to say they like the Common Core. A Kentucky teachers’ union leader said the foundation’s money could influence the state and federal education departments and both national teachers’ unions.
