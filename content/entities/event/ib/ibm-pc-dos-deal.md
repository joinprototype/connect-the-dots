In 1980 [[ibm]] needed an operating system for its first personal computer. [[microsoft]] signed the contract in November 1980 without having one, licensed [[86-dos]] from [[seattle-computer-products]], bought it outright in 1981 and delivered it as PC DOS. Keeping the right to sell it to other manufacturers as MS-DOS became the basis of Microsoft's business.

According to [[the-seattle-times]], [[mary-maxwell-gates]], who served on the United Way's national board with IBM's president [[john-opel]], helped cement the connection.
