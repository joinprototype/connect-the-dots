The account used is a local historical society’s, which quotes the press of the day. It says the Rockefeller group claimed 57% of the proxies, an estimate in February put Rockefeller’s own direct control at 15%, and the holders for Stewart outnumbered those for Rockefeller roughly two to one, but the large blocks of shares on the Rockefeller faction’s side won.
