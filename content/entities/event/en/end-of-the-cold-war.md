The [[cold-war]] wound down between the [[fall-of-the-berlin-wall]] in 1989 and the [[dissolution-of-the-soviet-union]] in 1991. Who and what ended it is contested: the [[reagan-library]] credits the [[strategic-defense-initiative]], while other accounts point to Soviet reform, Soviet economics or the republics' own choices.

According to the [[us-government]], the [[united-states]] won it.
