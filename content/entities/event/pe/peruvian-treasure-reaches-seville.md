The gold and silver counted by [[xerez-true-report-of-peru|Francisco de Xerez]] went through Panama and Nombre de Dios to Seville. His report describes the vases carried to the [[casa-de-la-contratacion]] and the rest of Hernando’s shipload packed into twenty-six boxes.
