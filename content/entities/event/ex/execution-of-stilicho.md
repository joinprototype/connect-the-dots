Zosimus says soldiers swore before a bishop that they would only detain [[stilicho]], then produced a second letter ordering his death. He says Stilicho forbade his armed supporters to rescue him. Olympius subsequently had Duterius and Petrus beaten to death after torture failed to extract accusations against Stilicho.
