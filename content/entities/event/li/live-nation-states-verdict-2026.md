A jury, in a trial 33 states and D.C. continued after the Justice Department and several states settled, found Live Nation and Ticketmaster liable on every antitrust claim put to it: monopolizing primary ticketing and large amphitheaters, and tying amphitheaters to promotion. It set a $1.72 overcharge per ticket at major concert venues in 22 states. Post-trial motions and remedies, including a possible breakup, had no ruling in reports to late October 2026.
