A jury, in a trial 34 states continued after the Justice Department and several states settled, found Live Nation and Ticketmaster liable on every antitrust claim put to it, with a $1.72 overcharge per ticket at major concert venues in 22 states. Post-trial motions and remedies, including a possible breakup, were pending at the last report we read.
