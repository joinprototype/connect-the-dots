In February 1987 magistrates issued warrants for [[paul-marcinkus]] and two lay managers of the [[institute-for-the-works-of-religion]]. A Vatican tribunal refused extradition, calling the charges "conjectural". On 17 July 1987 the [[italian-court-of-cassation]] invalidated the warrants.
