On 1 December 1991 voters in [[ukraine]] overwhelmingly approved independence and elected [[leonid-kravchuk]] president. A week later he signed the [[belovezha-accords]]. According to the historian [[serhii-plokhy]], the vote doomed Gorbachev's plans for a renewed union.
