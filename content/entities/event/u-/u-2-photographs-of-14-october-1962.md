On Sunday 14 October 1962 a cloudless morning let a U-2 fly over western [[cuba]]. Analysts at the CIA's photo center identified Soviet medium-range missiles in its pictures the next day. They began the [[cuban-missile-crisis]] for the [[us-government]].
