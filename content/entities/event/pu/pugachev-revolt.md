In 1773 Pugachev asked the Yaik Cossack army to serve him as it had served earlier rulers. In 1774 he offered former landlord peasants freedom from landlords and taxes, in a proclamation the University of North Carolina Greensboro edition dates to June and the Free Economic Society dates to late July. His own followers surrendered him, and the government executed him in Moscow in January 1775.
