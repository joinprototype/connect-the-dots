[[roberto-calvi]] was found dead on 18 June 1982, a day after his secretary fell to her death at [[banco-ambrosiano]]. A British inquest first returned suicide; a second returned an open verdict. A Rome court later acquitted the men tried for murder but, on appeal, wrote that he was killed.
