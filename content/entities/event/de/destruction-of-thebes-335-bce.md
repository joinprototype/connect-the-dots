The destruction preceded the crossing into Asia. Diodorus describes defenders choosing political freedom and Alexander implementing the allied council’s decree; Plutarch attributes the decision to intimidation and the complaints of allies. Both writers report the killing and enslavement centuries later. See [[alexander-the-great]], [[diodorus-library-of-history]] and [[plutarch-life-of-alexander]].
