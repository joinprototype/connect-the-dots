On 7 November 1917 the [[bolsheviks]], acting through the Petrograd Soviet's Revolutionary Military Committee chaired by [[leon-trotsky]], deposed the [[russian-provisional-government]]. [[vladimir-lenin]] became head of the new Soviet government.
