In April 1917 [[vladimir-lenin]] and some thirty other exiles travelled from [[zurich]] across Germany to Sweden and on to [[petrograd]]. Their carriage was treated as extraterritorial ("sealed").

Both sides recorded the deal. Lenin's group said the Swiss socialist Fritz Platten negotiated it with the German ambassador; the [[german-imperial-government]]'s files show the General Staff approving the transit. A German report later that month added: "He is working exactly as we would wish."
