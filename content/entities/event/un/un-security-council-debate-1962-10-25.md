On 25 October 1962 [[adlai-stevenson]] asked the Soviet ambassador, Valerian Zorin, in the [[un-security-council]] whether the USSR had placed missiles in [[cuba]]. Zorin did not answer. Stevenson then showed the council the U.S. photographs.
