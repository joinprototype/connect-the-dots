Project Vanguard was the US satellite program for the International Geophysical Year, led by the Naval Research Laboratory from 1955. Its first launch attempt failed in December 1957; Vanguard 1 reached orbit on 17 March 1958. Its staff became the nucleus of NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center.

It is unrelated to the investment firm [[the-vanguard-group]].
