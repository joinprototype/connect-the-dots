The FCC’s 2024 report said about 45 percent of households had no more than one provider of 100/20 Mbps service; the 2026 report, adopted 13 August 2026, put it at about 46 percent for fixed wireline, 23 percent counting fixed wireless and 3 percent counting satellite too.
