The French National Assembly’s modern historical account reports 642 of 718 present for guilt, 423 of 721 against appeal to the people, 361 of 721 for unconditional death plus 26 for death with a possible delay, and 380 against reprieve (no total given). Its majority-of-one shorthand describes the smallest absolute majority for unconditional death, not a 361–360 division between death and all alternatives. Liberty, Equality, Fraternity describes guilt as unanimous; its account differs. The original roll calls were not read. [National Assembly](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/histoire-et-patrimoine/revolution-francaise/proces-et-execution-de-louis-xvi), [Liberty, Equality, Fraternity](https://revolution.chnm.org/d/327).
