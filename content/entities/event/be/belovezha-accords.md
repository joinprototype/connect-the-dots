On 8 December 1991, at a hunting lodge in the Belovezha Forest in [[belarus]], [[boris-yeltsin]], [[leonid-kravchuk]] and [[stanislav-shushkevich]] declared that the USSR had ceased to exist and created the Commonwealth of Independent States. Gorbachev was not told in advance. US and Soviet records both tie the agreement to the end of the Union.
