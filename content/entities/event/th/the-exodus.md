The Exodus is the story, told in the [[book-of-exodus]], of how [[moses]] led the Israelites out of Egypt. It is a founding narrative of [[judaism]] and is also retold in [[christianity]] and [[islam]]. Its historicity is debated by scholars, so claims about it are attributed to the text.
