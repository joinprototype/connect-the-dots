After a bench trial in 2021 Apple won Epic’s Sherman Act claims (market for mobile-games transactions) and lost under California’s Unfair Competition Law on its ban on steering buyers elsewhere; in December 2025 the Ninth Circuit affirmed a finding that Apple had defied the resulting injunction; the Supreme Court granted review, argument set for 11 January 2027.
