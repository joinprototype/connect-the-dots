A jury found Google had unlawfully kept monopoly power in Android app distribution and in-app billing; the Ninth Circuit affirmed the verdict and the injunction on 31 July 2025. A settlement proposed in November 2025 was withdrawn on 15 July 2026, a class-action news site reports.
