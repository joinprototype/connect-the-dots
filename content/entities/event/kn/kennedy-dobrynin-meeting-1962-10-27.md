On the evening of 27 October 1962 [[robert-f-kennedy]] met Soviet ambassador [[anatoly-dobrynin]] at the Justice Department. Each wrote an account. Kennedy's memo says he refused any deal over the [[jupiter-missile]] missiles in [[turkey]]; Dobrynin's cable records him saying they would come out in four or five months. See [[jupiter-withdrawal-1963]].
