Amazon.com, Inc. was founded by [[jeff-bezos]] in 1994 and is based in [[seattle]]. Bezos was its chief executive until July 2021 and is now executive chair.

Its first outside investors were Bezos's parents, [[mike-bezos]] and [[jackie-bezos]], who bought shares worth about $245,573 in 1995. [[kleiner-perkins]] followed in 1996, and its partner [[john-doerr]] joined the board.
