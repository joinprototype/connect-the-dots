Keyhole was founded in 2001 and made EarthViewer 3D, which let users fly over satellite imagery. [[in-q-tel]] invested in February 2003 on behalf of the National Imagery and Mapping Agency, which used the software for US troops in Iraq. [[google]] bought Keyhole in October 2004.
