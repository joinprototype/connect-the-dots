Microsoft was founded in 1975 by [[bill-gates]] and [[paul-allen]] in [[albuquerque]], New Mexico, and moved to the Seattle area in 1979.

[[the-vanguard-group]] and [[blackrock]] each hold more than 5% of its shares for clients.

Its breakthrough was the 1980 [[ibm-pc-dos-deal]]: it supplied the operating system for the [[ibm-pc]] by buying [[86-dos]] from [[seattle-computer-products]], and kept the right to license it to others. In 2007 it bought a $240 million stake in [[meta-platforms|Facebook]].
