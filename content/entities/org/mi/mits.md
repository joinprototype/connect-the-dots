MITS made the Altair 8800, which appeared on the cover of Popular Electronics in January 1975. [[paul-allen]] became its vice-president of software and [[microsoft]] (then Micro-Soft) licensed Altair BASIC to it. In 1977 Gates and Allen ended the licence and the dispute went to arbitration.
