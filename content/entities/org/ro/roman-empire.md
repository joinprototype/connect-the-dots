The Roman Empire is the state this story follows. Its first emperor, [[augustus]], had his acts engraved as bringing "the whole world" under Rome. It was shared between two courts after [[division-of-the-empire-395]]; its western half is the subject of [[the-fall-of-rome-debate]].
