Seattle Computer Products wrote [[86-dos]] for its own 8086 computer kit. [[microsoft]] licensed it in December 1980 for $25,000 and bought all rights the next summer for $50,000, then supplied it to [[ibm]]. SCP later sued Microsoft and settled out of court for almost $1 million.
