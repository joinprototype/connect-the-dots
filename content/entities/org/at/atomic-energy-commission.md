The Atomic Energy Commission was created by the Atomic Energy Act of 1946 to put atomic energy under civilian control. It ran the US nuclear laboratories and weapons program until 1975, when its work passed to new agencies.

[[lawrence-preston-gise]], grandfather of [[jeff-bezos]], headed its operations in [[albuquerque]].
