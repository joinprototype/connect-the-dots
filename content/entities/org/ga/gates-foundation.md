Its fact sheet gives total giving to it by Bill Gates and Melinda French Gates as $63.9 billion through 2025, and by Warren Buffett as $47.9 billion from 2006 through 2025. It reports a $89 billion endowment at the end of 2025 and $8.47 billion of charitable support in 2025.
