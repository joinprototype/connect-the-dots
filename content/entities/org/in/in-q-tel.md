In-Q-Tel was set up by the [[cia]] in 1999 to invest in start-ups whose technology the intelligence community wants. It calls itself independent: "It’s not a government entity nor a part of the CIA."

Its 2003 investment in [[keyhole-inc|Keyhole]] became, after [[google]] bought Keyhole in 2004, the technology behind [[google-earth]]. It was also an early backer of [[palantir-technologies]].
