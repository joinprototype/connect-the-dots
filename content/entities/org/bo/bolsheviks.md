The Bolsheviks, led by [[vladimir-lenin]], seized power in the [[october-revolution]] of 1917. In March 1918 they renamed themselves the Russian Communist Party (Bolsheviks), later the [[communist-party-of-the-soviet-union]], which ruled until 1991. In this graph they are also a perspective: claims marked "according to the Bolsheviks" follow their own published statements.

The [[german-imperial-government]]'s own records show German money reaching them in 1917; the scale is debated.
