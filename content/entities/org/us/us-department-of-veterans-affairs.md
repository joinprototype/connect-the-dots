**1944–1945.** The Veterans Administration implemented Title III and issued instructions to private lenders.

> It is a responsibility and duty of the lenders to make certain that all veterans with whom they have dealings fully understand their rights and privileges under the Act and how to proceed to make application for a guaranteed loan.

[Servicemen’s Readjustment Act, Title III, §500(a)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/STATUTE-58/pdf/STATUTE-58-Pg284.pdf) · [Veterans Administration instructions to lenders, p. 1](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/historical/frbdal/circulars/frbdallas_circ_19450223b.pdf)
