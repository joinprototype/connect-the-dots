The Central Intelligence Agency was established by the National Security Act of 1947 and has been based in Langley, Virginia, since 1961. In 1999 it set up and funded [[in-q-tel]], a venture firm whose early investments included [[keyhole-inc|Keyhole]] (the basis of [[google-earth]]) and [[palantir-technologies]].
