Palantir was co-founded in 2003 by [[peter-thiel]], Alex Karp and others. Its 2020 prospectus says it "started building software for the intelligence community in the United States to assist in counterterrorism investigations and operations". [[in-q-tel]], the venture firm set up by the [[cia]], was among its early backers.
