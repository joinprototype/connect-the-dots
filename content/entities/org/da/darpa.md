The Advanced Research Projects Agency (ARPA) was established by the US Department of Defense on 7 February 1958, in response to [[sputnik-1]]. It was later renamed the Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency.

ARPA funded the [[arpanet]], whose first link went live in 1969.
