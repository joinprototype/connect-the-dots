Formed after the [[february-revolution]], the Provisional Government kept Russia in the war and was overthrown in the [[october-revolution]]. In July 1917 it accused [[vladimir-lenin]] of having been sent by the [[german-imperial-government]]; in this graph it is also a perspective.
