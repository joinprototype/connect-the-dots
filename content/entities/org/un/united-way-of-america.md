The national board of the United Way brought together business and civic leaders. Around 1980 it included IBM's president [[john-opel]] and Seattle's [[mary-maxwell-gates]], who chaired its executive committee from 1983. [[bill-gates]] later served on the same national board, and gives the proceeds of his 2025 memoir to United Way Worldwide.
