IBM visited Digital Research in 1980 about an operating system for its PC and the two made no deal ([[ibm-visit-to-digital-research-1980]]). The Computer History Museum puts the impasse down to price: IBM wanted to buy CP/M outright, Digital Research wanted a royalty on each copy. See [[ibm-pc-dos-deal]].
