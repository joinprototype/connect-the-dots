TIME’s reconstruction of [[banco-ambrosiano-collapse]] attributed [[roberto-calvi]]’s share purchases to an effort to strengthen control, and his use of IOR letters to reassuring directors of Ambrosiano’s Peruvian bank. Both interpretations appear in its 13 September 1982 report, “The Great Vatican Bank Mystery”.
