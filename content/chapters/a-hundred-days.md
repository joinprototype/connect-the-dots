---

Between April and July 1994, by estimates from 500,000 to more than a million, people were killed in Rwanda. This was genocide against the Tutsi, as the International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda found, and the UN's own inquiry found that the UN failed to stop it.

Nearly every line here comes from the cables, judgments and inquiries themselves, and from survivors and witnesses in their own words as the Tribunal and a 1999 television documentary recorded them. Where counts differ, the story says who counted. The UN, France, the U.S., Belgium and Rwanda's current government each get their say, and so do the men the Tribunal convicted.
