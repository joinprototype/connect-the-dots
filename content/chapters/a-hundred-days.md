summary: "Between April and July 1994 about 800,000 people were killed in Rwanda. A court found genocide; the UN’s own inquiry found the UN failed. Here is the record, from the cables, the judgments, the inquiries and the survivors’ own words."
---

Between April and July 1994, by estimates from 500,000 to more than a million, people were killed in Rwanda. This was genocide against the Tutsi, as the International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda found, and the UN's own inquiry found that the UN failed to stop it.

Before the killing, an informant offered to reveal an arms cache if UN peacekeepers would protect his family. During it, refugees clung to departing UN trucks. Officials argued over troops, mandates and the word genocide; survivors later testified against men who had ordered their families killed.
