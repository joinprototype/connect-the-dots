In 1939 a physicist warned a president about a new kind of bomb. Six years later it fell on two cities.

Nearly every line here comes from U.S. government histories, the writings of those who decided, and the museums of the two cities. Death tolls differ by who counted, so the story says who. The case for the bomb and the case against each get their full say, and the story does not pick one.
