---

Do members of Congress beat the stock market? The honest answer from the studies is that it depends on the years, the data and the method. This story follows them, and the law, the cases and the failed bills around them.

Every line comes from a study, a statute, a Justice Department release, a vote record or a newspaper's account. A good return is not evidence of a crime, and a disclosed trade is not either. Where a court or an agency found something, the story says so. Where people only suspected, it says who did.
