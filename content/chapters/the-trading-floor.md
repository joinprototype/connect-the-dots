---

Do members of Congress beat the stock market? An early Senate study found an edge of 85 basis points a month; a later study put average congressional portfolios 2 to 3% a year behind. The years, the people and the method change the answer. Meanwhile, particular trades have cost lawmakers their seats and brought prison sentences. Congress clarified the insider-trading prohibition in 2012, but its members can still own and trade stocks.
