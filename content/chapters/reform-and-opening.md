---

By the World Bank's figures, output (GDP) per person in China rose from about $157 in 1978 to about $12,951 in 2023, in current U.S. dollars, and almost 800 million people left extreme poverty on its line. China's own count uses a different line. The same years brought mass layoffs, a national limit on births with forced abortions on the record, and in 1989 troops firing on protesters in Beijing.

Farmers risked punishment to keep more of their harvest. Leaders opened markets while defending Party rule. Workers lost jobs, parents lost children, and families moved to cities that needed their labour but withheld public services. Their choices and losses run through the competing explanations of China’s rise.
