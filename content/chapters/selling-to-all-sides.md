A popular version says the U.S. “sponsored” the Nazis, Japan and the Soviets. This story treats that as a claim to test, not a fact.

The test is two questions. Who acted: the U.S. government, or a company? And when: before the U.S. entered the war in December 1941, or after? Each scene says which, gives each side of a dispute its say, and shows Antony Sutton’s thesis as his account, with what a historian found beside it. Some scenes cover only part of a deal, and some sources are a company’s or an author’s own account; the story says so.
