---

In 1870 Standard Oil was one refiner among many in Cleveland. In 1911 the Supreme Court ordered it broken up.

Nearly every line here comes from court opinions, sworn testimony, government reports, and the books of those who fought over the company, its founder's among them. Where counts differ, the story says who counted. The case for the company and the case against each get their say, and the story does not pick one.
