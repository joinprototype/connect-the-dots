---

Some say journalism is dead and owned by billionaires. This story tests both words against the record: what owners did, what they said, what studies found, and what reporters still publish, owners included.

In 2026 the ten largest newspaper owners controlled about two-thirds of local dailies, Medill found. Ownership changed endorsements, staffing and broadcasts; journalists also kept reporting on their employers and the people who could sue them. Those choices cost jobs, subscriptions and hundreds of millions of dollars.
