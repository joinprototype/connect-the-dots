---

Some say journalism is dead and owned by billionaires. This story tests both words against the record: what owners did, what they said, what studies found, and what reporters still publish, owners included.

Most lines come from a Senate report, a British public inquiry, academic studies, and news reporting, much of it NPR's, which is itself part of the press being tested. No court filing was read directly; court rulings come as the press reported them. Where accounts differ, the story says who said what and does not pick one. A settlement is not a finding unless a court or the party says so. Facts stop at the latest date in the sources, 1 November 2026.
