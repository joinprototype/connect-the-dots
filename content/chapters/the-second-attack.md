title: The Second Attack
subtitle: A Tonkin Gulf attack that was not found to have happened, and Iraqi weapons that were not found, each followed by a war, in the records of what each official was told
summary: In August 1964 two U.S. destroyers reported a second attack off North Vietnam, and Congress voted for the Tonkin Gulf Resolution. In 2003 the U.S. invaded Iraq after officials said it held banned weapons that were not found. Here is what the NSA, the Senate and the officials' own papers show each was told, and said, and when.
---

In August 1964 two U.S. destroyers reported a second attack off North Vietnam, and Congress voted for the Tonkin Gulf Resolution. In 2003 the U.S. invaded Iraq after officials said it held banned weapons. The second attack was not found to have happened, and the stockpiles were not found.

Johnson and Bush ordered force; sailors, intelligence officers and senators faced decisions of their own. Their telephone calls, warnings, speeches and later testimony show what they were told, the reasons they gave and what followed.
