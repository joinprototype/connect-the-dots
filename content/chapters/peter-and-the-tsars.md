Workers fled despite armed escorts, and in December 1712 the Senate reported the scale of the flight to Peter. Historian Robert K. Massie records that the officials and noblemen who sent the drafts warned Peter that losing men was ruining villages. Andreeva found 61 deaths among 2,210 skilled workers sent to settle permanently in 1711–12, and found that dispatch offices replaced deserters and sick workers, so their lists supply no citywide death count."
