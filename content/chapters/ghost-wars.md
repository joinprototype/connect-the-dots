---
In December 1979 Soviet troops entered Afghanistan. Over the next forty years the country saw the Soviet withdrawal, a Taliban government, and a U.S. war that ended in 2021. Along the way three claims grew: that the CIA made Osama bin Laden, that Saudi Arabia and the U.S. government funded the Taliban together, and that the U.S. made enemies in order to spend trillions. This story sets out what the sources record, what a named body found, and what is only alleged.

Most lines here come from the 9/11 Commission, State Department records, Human Rights Watch, SIGAR’s reports, and the words of those involved. Where counts differ, it says who counted.
