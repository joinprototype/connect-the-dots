title: The Red Rubber
subtitle: How Congolese testimony prompted an inquiry into forced rubber and reached diplomats weighing Belgium's takeover
summary: Roger Casement took Congolese accounts of forced rubber to London in 1903. The Congo Free State challenged his evidence, then its own commissioners confirmed abuses. Belgium took sovereignty in 1908, and many of the practices the witnesses described continued.
---

From 1885 to 1908, Leopold II governed the Congo Free State as its sovereign. He was also King of the Belgians. In the Congo, both the State and the companies it granted concessions collected rubber. The people who gathered it would tell a British consul and then the King's own commissioners what happened when they fell short.
