Here is what juries, appeals courts, regulators and the companies themselves said about who was hurt, who sued, what was paid and what was admitted."
---

Skin, eye and beauty products are used by millions of people, and when one goes wrong the proof arrives slowly, in studies, trials, court rulings and settlements.

Every line here is sourced: court records, government pages, journals, the companies' own words and established press. Where a company has replied, the story gives its reply. It keeps four things apart: what a study showed, what a jury found, what an appeals court changed, and what a company settled without admitting fault. A settlement is not a finding of guilt. A lawsuit is an allegation until a court decides. Nothing here is medical advice.
