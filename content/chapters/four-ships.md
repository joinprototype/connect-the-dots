title: From Four Ships to Portsmouth
subtitle: How Japan went from a closed country in 1853 to a power that beat Russia in 1905, and what it cost Taiwan, Korea and China
summary: In July 1853 four U.S. ships anchored off Uraga and the shogun's government was thrown into turmoil. By September 1905 Japan had a constitution, a war indemnity from China, Taiwan, an alliance with Britain and Russia's agreement not to hinder it in Korea. Here are the treaties, reports and archives that show the steps, who paid, and the reasons the sources give for why it worked.
---

In July 1853 four American ships anchored off Uraga, and, in the Library of Congress's words, the shogun's government "was thrown into turmoil." In September 1905 Russia signed a treaty saying Japan had "predominant political, military, and economic interests in Korea." Between those dates Japan wrote a constitution, won a war with the Qing empire, and took Taiwan and a war indemnity of 200,000,000 taels. In 1910 it annexed Korea.
