title: The Torah to the Press
subtitle: A Greek translation, Jerome’s return to Hebrew and the Latin Bible Gutenberg printed
summary: Gutenberg printed a text already changed by translators and copyists. Follow the Torah into his Latin Bible through the arguments over which words deserved to survive.
---

The Torah’s five books supply the thread: a law handed to readers, translated into Greek, translated from Hebrew into Latin by Jerome and carried within the Latin Bible to Mainz. The journey follows a text through its versions, rather than one physical manuscript through every century.
