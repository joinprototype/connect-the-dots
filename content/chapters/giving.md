Who gives secretly, and what does that conceal? Barre Seid transferred $1.6 billion to a political-advocacy trust. Robert Brulle traced $558 million in foundation grants to groups opposing carbon-emission limits, with much of their wider funding untraceable. Inside Philanthropy reports Susan Buffett's foundation gave over $5 billion, mostly for reproductive health and abortion rights; former program director Judith DeSarno said in 2008 that grants required anonymity after a boycott uproar.

Those decisions do not substantiate a blanket claim that billionaires' wives secretly fund environmental harm or inequality. They reveal a wider question: how much power does giving buy, who pays part of the bill, and who gets to see where the money goes?
