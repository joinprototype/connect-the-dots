A claim goes round that billionaires' wives secretly fund harm to the environment and to equality. This story found no published source for it, and tests it against named people: who gave, how much, through what vehicle, and what is public.

It is a narrow reading, not a full search. It rests on the tax code, IRS and Treasury pages, a peer-reviewed study, news reports, the givers' own words and their critics' and defenders'. Living people appear only for documented acts and their own words. A gift is not evidence of wrongdoing, and "harm" here is only ever a critic's word.
