title: The Bill for America
subtitle: What France lent and spent for American independence, what historians say it did to French finances, and the key of the Bastille at Mount Vernon
summary: France lent and spent to help win American independence, and eleven years after the alliance the Bastille fell. Here are the treaties, letters and accounts, and the differing readings of how much the money mattered. No source here shows that it alone brought down the king.
---

France signed an alliance with the United States in 1778 and lent money to win the war. In 1790 Lafayette sent George Washington the key of the Bastille.

This story rests on treaties, letters, diaries and accounts of the time, a school textbook, a teaching website of documents, and a few economic historians' articles, most of them read only as published abstracts. Their readings of what caused the French Revolution differ, and the story says who wrote what. No source here makes the whole chain from the alliance to the king's fall, and where the sources give no count, it gives none. It shows no pictures of executions or the dead.
