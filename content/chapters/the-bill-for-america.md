title: The Bill for America
subtitle: What France lent and spent for American independence, what historians say it did to French finances, and the key of the Bastille at Mount Vernon
summary: Louis XVI helped finance American independence, then faced a struggle over debts, taxes and royal power at home. Lafayette sent Washington the key of the Bastille; the king who had supported their war went to the guillotine.
---

France's 1782 contract with the United States recorded eighteen million livres in treasury loans and a guaranteed Dutch loan worth ten million more. France 24 puts the monarchy's total war spending above a billion livres. The American loans were a small part of the bill France carried home.

Louis XVI's ministers borrowed, offered reforms and fought over who should pay. Eleven years after the alliance of 1778, the Bastille fell; Lafayette sent its key to Washington, and the struggle over money and power went on.
