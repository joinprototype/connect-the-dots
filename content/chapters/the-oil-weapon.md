summary: In October 1973 the Arab members of OPEC cut output and stopped oil to the United States. Prices quadrupled and Americans queued for gasoline. Here is the road from OPEC's founding to the Strategic Petroleum Reserve, from declassified State Department records, statutes, and the words of those who were there.
---

In October 1973 a war in the Middle East brought an embargo on oil to the United States. By January 1974 the price of a barrel had nearly quadrupled, and in the winter that followed Americans queued at gas stations.
