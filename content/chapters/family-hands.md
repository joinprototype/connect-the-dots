title: Family Hands
subtitle: Who holds the big beauty and luxury companies, by what structure, and how much say each holder really has
summary: At some big beauty, eyewear and jewellery firms a family holds control, at some a large minority, at some nothing. Here are the holders as the firms and press report them, what regulators and courts did in the cases we read, and what each kind of owner does and does not do.
---

A popular version says a few families own the world's big consumer brands and use that to feed the public poor products. We did not trace where it comes from. The first half can be read in the reports of the listed firms here, and in press for the two private ones. At some a family holds control, at some a large minority, at some nothing. The second half is not a finding, and this story does not say it.

The firms were picked by us as large names in beauty, eyewear and jewellery. No source here gives any family's share of a market, so the list proves nothing about "a few families". Where a regulator or a court acted against a company, the story says what, for the cases in the sources we read. A holding is a fact; a connection is not evidence of wrongdoing.
