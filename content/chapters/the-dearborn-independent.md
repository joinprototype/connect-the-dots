title: The Dearborn Independent
subtitle: How a car maker’s newspaper carried a Russian forgery around the world, who paid for it, and what came of it
summary: In 1920 Henry Ford’s newspaper began printing a false, antisemitic series built on a forgery, the Protocols of the Elders of Zion. Here is how the lie was made, paid for and carried, how it was exposed in print and fought in court, what the record does and does not show about its reach to Hitler, and what his regime then did. From archives, a museum’s research, a Nuremberg transcript and the words of those who fought it.
---

The Protocols of the Elders of Zion is a forgery, and “The International Jew”, the series that Henry Ford’s newspaper built on it, is antisemitic propaganda whose charges are false. This story is about how that lie was made, paid for and carried, not about what it claimed.

Where sources differ, the story says who counted or who said so. The ADL and the American Jewish Committee took part in the fight over this paper, and some of what follows comes from their own accounts. How far Ford shaped Hitler is not settled: the story gives what is documented and each view with its owner, and it found no historian who limits Ford’s part.
