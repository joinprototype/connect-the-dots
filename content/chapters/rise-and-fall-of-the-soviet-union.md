subtitle: Who sponsored the Bolsheviks and why, who gained, and why the Union fell, as each side told it
summary: Imperial Germany's own files show it helped Lenin home and paid for Bolshevik propaganda. Seventy-four years later Washington, Gorbachev and the Kremlin each blamed something different for the collapse.
---

Textbooks often tell the Soviet story as a revolution from below in 1917 and a sudden collapse in 1991. The records show more hands at work. This chapter follows who helped the Bolsheviks to power and what they wanted in return, then the accounts of why the Union fell, side by side.

Lines marked "according to" are one side's account. Where records from opposing sides agree, the link is shown as confirmed by both.
