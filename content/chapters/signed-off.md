summary: Companies pay the firms that audit them. GAO’s 2008 report found four firms audited 98% of more than 1,500 U.S. public companies with annual revenue over $1 billion. Here is the record from the Big Eight to the Big Four, from Enron and the Supreme Court to Lehman, Wirecard, Carillion and Evergrande, with each firm’s own reply and what each case did and did not decide.
---

Companies pay their auditors. GAO’s 2008 report found four firms audited 98% of more than 1,500 U.S. public companies with annual revenue over $1 billion. Here is what regulators, courts and the firms themselves said when their audits were challenged.
