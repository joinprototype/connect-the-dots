---

Who pays U.S. antitrust enforcers? Congress sets their budgets, and companies proposing large mergers must pay filing fees. The Division’s recent budgets count on those fees for its whole appropriation; the FTC also draws on the general fund.

Lobbying, campaign contributions and donations to policy research follow different paths. The people who wrote the laws, reviewed the mergers and disputed the funding explain what they chose—and whose money was at stake.
