summary: "A claim in circulation says U.S. antitrust work is fully funded by monopolies and their donations. The agencies' own papers show a more exact picture: fees set by law, paid by every company that files a large merger, make up the Antitrust Division's whole appropriation by its own estimate and most of the FTC's money. Here are the statutes, the budget tables, the lobbying records, and each side's answer where the sources carry one."
---

A claim in circulation says U.S. antitrust enforcement is fully funded by monopolies and their donations. This story tests it line by line.

Nearly every number comes from the agencies’ own budget papers, the statutes, lobbying reports filed with the Senate, or court records. Where a newspaper is the source, the story says so. Where the sources here carry an answer from a company, a think tank or an agency, it is given in its own words; where one was not found, the scene says so. Nothing here says a payment bought a decision: no court or inquiry cited found that.
