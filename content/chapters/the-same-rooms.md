Larry Fink runs BlackRock. Elon Musk runs Tesla and SpaceX. Ken Griffin founded Citadel and is the principal shareholder of Citadel Securities. All three are living, and the first finding is a null one: no record we found puts all three in one room.

What follows is each pair, in date order, from documented acts and their own public words, and it says plainly where only their firms meet. Where a source gives only a plan or a report, it says so. A connection is not evidence of wrongdoing.
