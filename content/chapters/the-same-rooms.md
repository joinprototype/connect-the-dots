---

Larry Fink runs BlackRock. Elon Musk runs Tesla and SpaceX. Ken Griffin founded Citadel and is the principal shareholder of Citadel Securities. All three are living. A meeting of all three is not established; their connections run pair by pair.

A presidential adviser’s seat, a vote on an executive’s pay, a question about who stopped customers buying stock: each involved people choosing what to do. Some choices belonged to the men themselves, others to people working at their firms. A connection is not evidence of wrongdoing.
