After the Pentagon Papers came out, the White House set up a small unit to stop leaks. Three years later the President of the United States resigned.

Nearly every line here comes from the Special Prosecutor's report, court opinions, Senate hearings and the White House tapes. A charge is an accusation, not a finding of guilt: where a court decided something, the story says which court and when.
