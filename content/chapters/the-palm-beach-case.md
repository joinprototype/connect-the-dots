summary: In 2008 Epstein pleaded guilty to two Florida charges and served under 13 months. In 2019 New York prosecutors charged him again; he died in custody before trial. Here is the road between, from court opinions, the Justice Department's own reports, and the words of the women at the centre of it.
---

In 2008 Jeffrey Epstein pleaded guilty to two Florida charges and served less than 13 months of an 18-month term. Under a federal agreement, the U.S. Attorney's Office in southern Florida had agreed to end its own investigation, in which a prosecutor had listed 34 minors as victims. In 2019 prosecutors in New York charged him with sex trafficking of minors. He died in custody five weeks after his arrest, before any trial.

The sources read here show no court setting the 2008 agreement aside. A court later held, in Ghislaine Maxwell's case, that it covered only the Southern District of Florida.

The women who were girls at the time come first, in their own words. Nothing here describes what was done to them. Nearly every line comes from court opinions, the Justice Department's own reports, and the words of those who lived it. Where sources differ, the story says who said what. Read to 2 November 2026.
