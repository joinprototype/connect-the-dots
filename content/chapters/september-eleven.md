On the morning of 11 September 2001 al Qaeda hijackers flew airliners into the World Trade Center and the Pentagon, and a fourth crashed in Pennsylvania; the 9/11 Commission counted 2,973 dead. The official record of who did it, and of what went wrong before, is the Commission's report.

Nearly every line here comes from that report and the staff study on terrorist financing, from U.S. government statements, court coverage and the press. Where a later source goes beyond the Commission, the story says so and gives each side its say. Allegations are told as allegations, with who made them. The last scenes tell a hoax and the State Department's answer to it. No scene links one fact to another unless a source does.
