The events of 1948 are the War of Independence to Israelis and the Nakba, “the catastrophe”, to Palestinians. Between 700,000 and 750,000 Palestinians became refugees that year, by Avi Shlaim's estimate; Israel lost about 1 percent of its population, by Benny Morris's count. The fighting left Israel controlling about 77 percent of Mandatory Palestine, in Rashid Khalidi's calculation. The proposed Arab state did not come into existence.

This story follows the people who built settlements, promised independence, ordered expulsions, signed agreements and sought justice, from Ottoman Palestine to the courts at The Hague. It follows events through 26 October 2026, with the conflict still continuing.
