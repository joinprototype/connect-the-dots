---

The events of 1948 are the War of Independence to Israelis and the Nakba, "the catastrophe", to Palestinians. This story follows how the State of Israel came about and what has happened since, in the words of the people and bodies who were there, and of the historians who disagree about it.

The story uses the names its sources use, and gives both where they differ. Where historians, governments or courts disagree, each is quoted in its own words. A court's finding is said exactly: what it ruled, by what vote, and what it has not decided. Violence against civilians by any party is stated from the record. Numbers come with who counted and when; where a count is disputed, the story says who disputes it.

These sources run to 26 October 2026. The events do not stop there. The Ottoman registers, Arab government archives, and most of Israel's own full statements were not among the sources read; where Israel's position appears, it is as the courts, the State Department or its own ministries record it.
