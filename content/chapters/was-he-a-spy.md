Jeffrey Epstein harmed over one thousand victims, the Justice Department and the FBI say. That is what the survivors ask the government to answer for. This story is about a different question: whether he worked for an intelligence service. It is told as a claim, not as a fact, as read on 2 November 2026.

This claim is used to spread an antisemitic myth, as the Anti-Defamation League says. That myth is false. How such a lie was made and carried is told in [The Dearborn Independent](/c/the-dearborn-independent/). The Mossad is an agency of one government, not a people.

This story names only people who held public office, ran large institutions, were charged, or spoke publicly in their own name. A contact on the record, such as a meeting or a payment, is not evidence of knowledge or wrongdoing, and no one named here is said to have done wrong because of a link.
