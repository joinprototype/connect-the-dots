---

American home prices peaked in 2006. Two years later Lehman Brothers was bankrupt and Congress was voting on $700 billion.

Nearly every line here comes from the Financial Crisis Inquiry Report, the court examiner's report on Lehman, and the Federal Reserve's and Treasury's own words. A charge is an accusation, not a finding of guilt: where a case ended, the story says how. The commission could not agree on why it happened, so the majority and those who dissented each get their say.
