subtitle: Enron, Theranos and FTX, from what courts found, and what the boards, auditors and investors around them did and said
summary: Three companies fell in twenty years, and courts convicted the people who ran them. This story reads court opinions, the Powers Report, the Senate report, Justice Department and SEC releases and the FTX bankruptcy declaration for what the people around the convicted did and said, and what the collapses cost the people below them.
---

Enron fell in 2001, Theranos from 2015 and FTX in 2022. In each, a court convicted people who ran the company. Around them stood boards, auditors and investors, and below them the employees, patients and customers who paid.

Nearly every line comes from court opinions, committee reports, government releases and the people's own words. News reports and one side's own statements are named as such. Living people appear only through documented acts and their own public words, and each convicted person's denial stands beside any apology. Convictions still on appeal are marked. Where the records read here are silent, the story says so. A civil settlement is not a finding of guilt, and a charge is not a verdict.
