summary: Three companies fell in twenty years, and courts convicted executives of fraud. Directors and investors backed them; employees, patients and customers bore the damage. Their testimony, warnings and denials show what they chose and what followed.
---

Enron fell in 2001, Theranos from 2015 and FTX in 2022. In each, a court convicted people who ran the company. Around them stood boards, auditors and investors, and below them the employees, patients and customers who paid.

A director put $85,000 of his savings behind a blood-testing machine. A patient waited three months to afford a test that could tell her whether the first result was wrong. An investor asked a judge what chance he had when people inside the company were lying. Their choices run through the promises, warnings, denials and verdicts that followed.
