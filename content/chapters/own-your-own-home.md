summary: Federal campaigns asked Americans to buy homes. During the Depression, Washington helped turn a loan that came due in a few years into decades of monthly payments. At the Levittown sales office, veterans found out who could buy on those terms.

In 1919 the U.S. Department of Labor urged families to prepare to own a home. Thirty years later, a Long Island builder advertised one to veterans for $58 a month.
