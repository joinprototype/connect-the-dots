---

Fifteen skin-care brands lead to six companies. Their founders sold creams in salons; later executives bought brands and shareholders paid for the growth. Follow the people who made the promises, the customers who challenged them, and the patients and volunteers whose experiences put the jars to a test.

Sunscreen trials found benefits against some skin cancers. Other products brought unproven claims, contamination, lawsuits and lasting injuries; each allegation, finding and reply belongs to the person or body making it.
