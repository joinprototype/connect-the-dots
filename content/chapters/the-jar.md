La Roche-Posay Anthelios Wet Skin SPF 50+ met its label in the batch tested, one of four products to pass in CHOICE’s June 2025 comparison of twenty products. The other sixteen fell short. This bottle leads from a familiar brand to the people who own the business, the rules behind its promises, and a test that brought manufacturers’ assurances under scrutiny.
