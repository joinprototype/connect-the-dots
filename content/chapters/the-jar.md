Walk down a skin-care aisle and the jars look like rivals. This story follows the record: who owns the brands, what regulators and courts did about the claims on the label, what has been alleged or tested as harmful, and what trials show works.

Every number comes from a company report, a regulator, a court, a published study, a consumer group or a library exhibit, or is our own count, marked as such. A charge is not a finding of guilt, a holding is not wrongdoing, and nothing here is medical advice. Nothing here is a reason to stop using sunscreen.
