On 29 October 2018 and 10 March 2019, two Boeing 737 MAX planes crashed minutes after takeoff. 346 people died. The story follows what courts and investigators found, and what Boeing, the FAA and the families said in reply.

Boeing admitted deceiving the FAA’s pilot-training group. The families fought to have a say in the criminal case; prosecutors made successive agreements with the company, and courts set limits on what judges could decide. Investigators in Ethiopia and the U.S. still disagree about the failed sensor and the crew’s actions. The question of how far a regulator leans on the industry it oversees also runs through [[user-fees]].
