subtitle: Does the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission enforce the rules? Its own inspectors, auditors, judges and Congress, and its replies
summary: Twenty decisions behind the SEC’s enforcement record, from Roosevelt’s new commission to missed frauds, large settlements and fights over how to regulate.
---

The SEC has won insider-trading cases and secured settlements including $550 million from Goldman Sachs. Its own inspectors also found that it repeatedly missed Madoff's fraud and left Stanford's suspected Ponzi scheme unpursued for years.

These 20 moments follow the people who warned, investigated, settled and chose how to regulate, from Roosevelt's new commission to the cases filed in fiscal 2025. A connection is not evidence of wrongdoing.
