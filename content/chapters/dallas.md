---

On 22 November 1963 President Kennedy was shot in Dallas. Official inquiries have given different answers about whether anyone else was involved. No official inquiry found that the CIA took part; several found it held things back, and one CIA officer's role is still disputed.

Kennedy had faced a failed invasion of Cuba and publicly honored the CIA director who left afterward. After his murder, officials investigated Oswald while keeping assassination plots against Castro secret. Their decisions shaped what the inquiries could establish—and what later generations would ask to see.
