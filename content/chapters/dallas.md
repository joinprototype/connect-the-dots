---

On 22 November 1963 President Kennedy was shot in Dallas. Official inquiries have given different answers about whether anyone else was involved. No official inquiry found that the CIA took part; several found it held things back, and one CIA officer's role is still disputed.

Nearly every line here comes from the Warren Commission, the Church Committee, the House Select Committee on Assassinations, the National Archives and the CIA's own history. A popular remark about Kennedy and the CIA is traced to its source. The story does not say who was behind the killing beyond what an official inquiry found, and where they disagree, each gets its say.
