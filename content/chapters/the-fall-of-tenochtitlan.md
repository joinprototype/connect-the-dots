subtitle: The fall of Tenochtitlan, 1519 to 1521
summary: Moctezuma welcomed Hernán Cortés to Tenochtitlan. The city drove Cortés out, then faced his return with Indigenous allies and a fleet. Cuauhtémoc was taken on 13 August 1521, after a siege Cortés counted at seventy-five days.
---

On 8 November 1519, Moctezuma met Hernán Cortés with flowers and gold necklaces. Less than two years later, after a siege Cortés counted at seventy-five days, Cuauhtémoc faced him as a prisoner and people came out of the city hungry. Book 12 of the Florentine Codex, written down in Nahuatl decades later, remembers the welcome and the siege from inside Tenochtitlan.
