---

Universities hold some of the largest pools of money in private hands. It is often said that these endowments send money back to their donors, untaxed. That claim is common; the nearest sourced version here is an anonymous 2012 letter about Dartmouth, told below as an allegation. This story follows the money to test it: what a gift is in law, what the funds pay for, who manages them, and where the record shows donors, trustees and managers touching them.

It rests on statutes, court opinions, the schools' own reports, congressional papers and the press. Where figures come from a school, the story says so. The defenders' case and the critics' case each get their say, and where the sources are silent, the story says that too.
