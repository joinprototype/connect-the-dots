title: The Great Dying
subtitle: Two envoys, hospital blankets and the British officers’ paper trail at Fort Pitt
summary: At Fort Pitt in 1763, British personnel gave two Delaware envoys articles from a smallpox hospital. A journal, letters and an invoice establish a deliberate attempt to spread disease; testimony records deaths without proving which infections the blankets caused. The wider population loss and the genocide debate follow that investigation.
---

Turtle’s Heart and Mamaltee asked the British to leave Fort Pitt. The British refused, then gave them articles from a smallpox hospital. William Trent recorded the gift; officers certified and paid the expense. Amherst and Bouquet discussed deliberate infection after the gift had already been made.

Fort Pitt is the thread because its journal, correspondence, account and testimony preserve the decisions in unusual detail. The envoys’ visit leads to the hospital gifts, the officers’ proposals and the army’s payment. Witnesses then describe the deaths; population estimates and the argument over genocide put that local evidence against the wider catastrophe.

His team pooled 119 published regional population estimates, without judging their relative quality. Studies they reviewed put losses between 40 and 95 percent. These are reconstructions from later censuses, tribute records, settlements and other evidence. Their central decline predates Fort Pitt by more than a century; it cannot be attributed to the 1763 attempt."

  narration: "**Can the deaths be called genocide?** The estimated toll leaves the question of intent. The 1948 Genocide Convention requires intent to destroy a protected group, wholly or partly; it includes killing, serious harm, destructive living conditions, prevention of births and forced transfer of children. In his 2004 reply to David Stannard, Guenter Lewy excluded inadvertent epidemic deaths from that definition, while allowing that some massacres could qualify. Stannard, as Lewy quotes him, counted people killed by introduced disease among victims of genocidal war. Fort Pitt supplies explicit intent to infect; the hemisphere’s missing millions cannot by themselves establish the intentions behind each death."
