---

In 1206 the Mongol people gathered at the source of the Onon river and gave a steppe chieftain the title of qan. Within about two generations his family ruled lands from the Pacific to the Black Sea, and the empire was splitting between his grandsons.

Here is the road, in the words of those who wrote it down: the Mongols' own Secret History, Persian, Arabic, Syriac, Rus and Latin chroniclers, friars who travelled there, and Marco Polo, whose book is disputed. Most of them wrote as people the Mongols conquered, served or visited. The death tolls are the medieval writers' own numbers. Dates use the modern calendar.
