summary: "From the assembly of 1206 to the Khwarazm war: the caravan seized at Otrar, Chinggis Khan’s decision, Bukhara, Samarkand, the shah’s flight and death, and Merv. Juvaini and Ibn al-Athir give different counts and explanations."
---

This story follows one war: Chinggis Khan’s campaign against Muhammad, the Khwarazm shah. Juvaini records 450 merchants sent west and killed on the shah’s authority; the Secret History gives Chinggis a hundred murdered envoys as his reason for revenge. The war took cities, treasure and forced labour, and at Merv Tolui’s army killed inhabitants who had surrendered. The question is why, and whose numbers tell us how many died.

Juvaini wrote in Mongol service a generation later. Ibn al-Athir wrote outside their rule, before his death in 1233. Their reported counts and reasons belong to each writer; the story follows their decisions and evidence as well as the campaign.
