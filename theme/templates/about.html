Shown apart, with the reason, and never counted.

A withdrawn link is never used. It stays listed, marked, on its entry's own page.

A connection is not evidence of wrongdoing

A link says that two things are connected and how we know. It does not say why, that anyone meant anything by it, or that anyone did wrong.

How routes are ranked

Shorter routes through well-backed links come first. Links that rest on weaker sources, and steps through very connected hubs, count as longer, so the most specific routes rise to the top. The rule is public and versioned{% if methodology %} (methodology {{ methodology }}){% endif %}. Links that are one side's account of who gained or lost never become steps in a route.

Families

Family ties are shown only for public figures in their public roles, and only with a primary record or two independent sources. No minors, and no relatives without a public role.

Something missing or wrong?

Every page links to the file it comes from. Propose a source or a correction with a pull request to that file on GitHub, or tell us in an issue. Every change is reviewed, and every page keeps its history.
